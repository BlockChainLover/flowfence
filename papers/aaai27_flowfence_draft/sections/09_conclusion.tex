
\section{Conclusion}

Multi-agent LLM privacy failures are best understood as propagation failures across runtime channels, not only as unsafe final outputs. FlowFence-Lite provides a practical non-oracle containment layer that mediates events, rewrites safe views, quarantines risky content, and incorporates topology and privilege signals. Across adapted retrieval-memory evaluation, deterministic synthetic MAS benchmarks, a MiniMax-backed multi-agent synthetic-runtime coverage experiment, and non-oracle held-out validation, the committed evidence supports scoped containment claims with explicit boundaries. The next step is to test beyond this setting: additional providers, real browser/desktop environments, stronger baselines, deeper ablations, and learned propagation-risk models.
