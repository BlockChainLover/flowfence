
\section{Limitations}

The evidence is intentionally scoped. MiniMax is the only real provider used, and MiniMax is used as a final writer inside a synthetic deterministic multi-agent runtime. We do not claim non-MiniMax generalization, production safety, real browser/desktop/computer-use deployment, or arbitrary attack robustness. The runtime does not model operating-system compromise, browser compromise, network attacks, human workflows, or real enterprise deployment.

The P0 retrieval-memory result is an adapted comparator, not an official AgentPoison reproduction. The non-oracle held-out validation covers configured paraphrases, not every possible paraphrase or adaptive attack. The no-semantic-pattern ablation is partial mechanism evidence, not a complete module-level ablation. FlowFence-Lite also does not yet include a learned graph-risk scorer.

Raw traces and provider outputs are intentionally not committed. This improves privacy discipline but limits independent qualitative inspection. The committed artifacts contain high-level summaries, tables, and redacted evidence. The qualitative examples are redacted safe-trace illustrations, not raw transcripts and not additional experiments; they improve interpretability but do not expand the evaluated matrix.

Future work should expand providers, add real computer-use environments, evaluate larger agent populations, deepen module-level ablations, and replace the hand-built scorer with a learned or calibrated graph-risk model where appropriate.
